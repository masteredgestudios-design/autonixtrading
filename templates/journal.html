Welcome to the Autonix Trading Routine

Autonix Trading is built on a simple belief: many traders lose money not because they have a poor strategy, but because they overtrade, become greedy after a win, or chase losses after a losing session.

Our objective is to help you develop a trading routine that emphasises consistency, patience, and responsible risk management. The goal is not to trade all day — it is to follow your plan, complete your session, and step away until your next scheduled opportunity.

Rule 1

🛡️ The 50% Take Profit Rule

Every trading session begins with one rule:

Set your Take Profit (TP) to exactly 50% of your selected stake.

Rule 2

🕒 Four Planned Trading Sessions

Divide your trading day into four planned sessions. Each session is a separate opportunity.

4:00 AM – 7:00 AM

🌙

11:00 PM – 2:00 AM

If you complete your planned session, stop and wait for the next one. Never restart another trading cycle during the same session.

Rule 3

✅ One Trading Cycle Per Session

Your greatest advantage is consistency — not the number of trades you place.

1Select your preferred trading bot
2Choose your stake
3Set your Take Profit to 50% of your stake
4Begin trading
5End the session once your target is reached or your risk plan says stop
6Record your results in your journal
7Wait for the next scheduled session

The Autonix Commitment

Every time I trade, I commit to:

  • Following my trading plan
  • Setting my Take Profit to 50% of my stake
  • Completing only one trading cycle per session
  • Trading no more than four planned sessions each day
  • Avoiding greed and revenge trading
  • Respecting that trading involves risk and profits are never guaranteed
  • Focusing on long-term consistency instead of short-term excitement

Discipline creates habits. Habits build consistency. Consistency supports long-term success.
